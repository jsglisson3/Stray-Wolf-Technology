Privacy

The short version: this site only collects what you send, and only uses it to reply to you and plan your project.

What this site collects

Only what you send. When you answer the questions on the Start a project page, that’s your name, your business name, your email and anything else you choose to add, like a phone number, a link or a note.

How it’s used

To reply to you and plan your project. That’s it. Your details aren’t sold or shared for marketing, and you won’t be added to a mailing list.

Where it’s kept

The form is delivered by Web3Forms, a form service, which passes your answers on to Jeremy’s email. What Web3Forms itself keeps is covered by the privacy policy on web3forms.com. If the form opens your email app instead, your answers go straight from your email to Jeremy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub keeps basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service secure. This site uses no cookies, ads or tracking.

Deleting your details

Reply to any email from Jeremy and ask. Anything Stray Wolf Technology has from you will be deleted.
