Good to know before we start.

Something else on your mind? The last box on the Start a project page is for exactly that.

How much does a website cost?

Starter sites begin at $1,500 and larger ones at $3,000; the Pricing page has the details. Want to see something first? A mockup is $50 to $100, and it comes off the price if we go ahead. After we talk you get one price in writing, before anything starts. No tiers, no surprise add-ons.

Do you take every job?

No. I’m not trying to take on as much work as I can, or make as much money as I can. I take the jobs I know I can do well, a few at a time, and I turn down the ones I don’t think I can do right. If yours isn’t a fit, I’ll say so up front and point you somewhere better.

How long does it take?

About 30 days from the time we agree to start. If you have a date that matters, put it in the form and I’ll tell you up front whether it works.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain, your content and your site are yours to keep.

What do you need from me?

Three things, and they’re your 10%: a clear idea of what you want, the words for each page, and your photos and logo. The other 90% is mine. The 30 days starts when I have them. No logo or photos yet? Say so and we’ll work it out.

Let’s talk about yours.

Six quick questions, about two minutes. You’ll hear from me within five days, with a price in writing before anything starts.
