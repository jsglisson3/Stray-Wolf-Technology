Good to know before we start.

How much does a website cost?

Starter sites from $1,500, larger ones from $3,000. Details on the Pricing page. Want to see something first? A mockup is $50 to $100, and if you go ahead, every dollar of it comes off your final price. One price in writing, before anything starts.

Do you take every job?

No. I take the jobs I know I can do well, a few at a time. If yours isn’t a fit, I’ll say so up front and point you somewhere better.

How long does it take?

About 30 days from yes. If you have a date that matters, put it in the form.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain, your content and your site are yours to keep.

Will my site show up on Google?

It’s built to. Every site gets the SEO basics: clear titles and descriptions, your business details in the format search engines read, a sitemap and fast pages. Then I submit it to Google and Bing. DuckDuckGo and Yahoo draw on Bing, so that covers them too. Nobody can honestly promise a ranking, so I don’t.

What do you need from me?

To start, just a conversation. Once I take the job, I’ll need your logo, as many photos as you can, and the words that matter most: your main headline or slogan, key phrases, wording or other guidelines. I’ll write the rest. I’ll send a short list of how to send it all. The 30 days starts when I have it.
