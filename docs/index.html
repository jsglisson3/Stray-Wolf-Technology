Good to know before we start.

Something else on your mind? The last box on the Start a project page is for exactly that.

How much does a website cost?

Starter sites begin at $1,800 and larger ones at $3,500; the Pricing page has the details. After we talk you get one price in writing, before anything starts. No tiers, no surprise add-ons.

Do you take on smaller jobs?

Yes. A fix on the site you have, one new page, or a refresh of your photos and words. Smaller jobs take days, not weeks, and start at $150. Same rule as everything else: a price in writing first.

How long does it take?

About 30 days from the time we agree to start. If you have a date that matters, put it in the form and I’ll tell you up front whether it works.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain, your content and your site are yours to keep.

What do you need from me?

Three things, and they’re your 10%: a clear idea of what you want, the words for each page, and your photos and logo. The other 90% is mine. The 30 days starts when I have them. No logo or photos yet? Say so and we’ll work it out.

Can I make changes after it’s live?

Yes. Small changes and fixes are quoted per job, from $150, and done within days. There’s no monthly fee and no contract: the site is yours, and the hosting I set up costs nothing a month.

Do you work outside my area?

Yes. The whole project runs over calls, email and shared links, so where you’re based doesn’t matter.

Do you do social media or Google Business Profiles?

No. I stick to websites, domains, hosting and email, and do them well.
