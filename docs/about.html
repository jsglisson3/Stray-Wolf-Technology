Who you’ll work with

A stray wolf doesn’t run with a pack, and neither do I. I build websites on my own, for businesses that want to stand out.

Who it’s for

Owner-run businesses anywhere in the US: the shop, the trade, the practice, the one-truck outfit. Most people find me through someone I’ve already built a site for.

I only take work by referral, and only a few clients at a time, so every site gets my full attention. If you’re a franchise with a head office, a large online store, or you need a full agency team, I’m probably not the right fit, and I’ll say so up front.

What I do

  • Website design and build

    By hand, for your business. No templates, no page builders.

  • Domain registration and setup

    I register it in your name and point it at your site.

  • Hosting and small updates

    After launch I keep the site up and make the small changes as they come.

  • Email at your own domain

    An address like you@yourbusiness.com, set up and handed over to you.

Google Business Profile and social media aren’t on the list. I stick to what I’m good at.

How I work

  • Hand-built for your business. No templates, no page builders.
  • I get to know you and what you want out of a website before I design anything.
  • Everything in plain English, no jargon.
  • A price in writing before anything starts.
  • Nothing goes live until you’ve seen it and said yes.

The internet is full of giants, and a small business can get lost in their shade. My job is to build the site that stands out among them.
